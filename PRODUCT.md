# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Conference attendees use CINDER before and during an event to meet relevant people and participate in small groups. Prospective attendees use the public site to understand the experience and join a conference.

## Product Purpose

CINDER helps conferences become more connected by helping attendees discover people, form Pods, communicate, and coordinate in-person meetups.

## Positioning

CINDER is social infrastructure for conferences. It combines attendee discovery, curated small-group Pods, persistent conversation, agenda context, and an AI facilitator around the goal of meaningful in-person connection.

## Operating Context

Attendees use CINDER on the web before and during conferences, often on mobile while moving between sessions and in-person conversations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current attendee capabilities include profile check-in, discovery recommendations, mutual connections, Pod conversation, meetup coordination, agenda browsing, and facilitator assistance.
- Preserve the existing API contracts and attendee workflows.
- The current React app has no separate authentication or organizer administration experience. Public calls to join or log in must lead to an existing attendee entry point.
- Do not imply unsupported organizer features.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the CINDER name and existing mark.
- The confirmed proposition is “Discover your people. Find your Pod. Make something happen.”
- The public site should lead with human connection and in-person participation, not AI as the product or conference administration.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains conference seed data and a working attendee application.
- Three supplied images depict conference conversation, a Pod lounge, and an in-person meetup.
- No verified testimonials, customer endorsements, partner logos, or participation metrics are provided. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Help people make meaningful connections in the room.
- Make discovery and small-group participation understandable before check-in.
- Keep attendee actions focused on what is happening next.
- Use the facilitator to support human interaction.
- Preserve working backend behavior and describe only capabilities present in the product.
