Now we're getting into the actual product presentation. 🔥

I want to make one important distinction for this commission: CINDER needs two complementary experiences, not one overloaded landing page.

1. The public landing page: sells the experience, establishes trust, explains the concept, and converts visitors into attendees.
2. The authenticated home experience: helps an attendee navigate their conference, discover people, participate in Pods, and coordinate meetups.

They should share one visual identity, but serve different purposes.

I'm going to commission this as a complete website and product-presentation redesign, covering the marketing site, header, hero, CTAs, social proof, product storytelling, footer, and the transition into the actual application.

# MAJO COMMISSION — CINDER

## PUBLIC WEBSITE & SOCIAL APPLICATION PRESENTATION

### LANDING PAGE · HEADER · HERO · NAVIGATION · CONVERSION · FOOTER · AUTHENTICATED HOME

**MISSION:** Transform CINDER's public-facing website and home presentation into a polished, distinctive, world-class social application experience.

**SCOPE:** Frontend UI, visual design, information architecture, landing-page composition, navigation, interaction ergonomics, and presentation.

**BACKEND:** Preserve all existing working backend functionality, integrations, API contracts, and business logic.

**EXECUTION:** Investigate, design, generate visual concepts, implement, inspect, and refine the actual application.

# 01 — THE CENTRAL PRODUCT POSITIONING

CINDER is social infrastructure for conferences.

It helps attendees:

- Discover interesting people.
- Find relevant connections.
- Join intelligently composed Pods.
- Communicate in dedicated group spaces.
- Coordinate physical meetups.
- Receive assistance from AI facilitators.
- Participate more meaningfully in conference activities.

The website must communicate this immediately.

Do not position CINDER as another event-management dashboard.

Do not position it as a dating application.

Do not present it as a generic AI chatbot.

CINDER is a social experience that helps conferences become more connected.

### Brand proposition

**DISCOVER YOUR PEOPLE. FIND YOUR POD. MAKE SOMETHING HAPPEN.**

Supporting proposition:

*The people you came to meet shouldn't be left to chance.*

The website should make visitors understand what CINDER does within seconds.

# 02 — FIRST INVESTIGATE THE EXISTING WEBSITE

Before implementing the redesign, inspect the complete current public-facing experience.

Identify:

- Existing landing page.
- Current homepage.
- Existing navigation and routes.
- Authentication entry points.
- Brand identity.
- Typography and color system.

Build the appropriate presentation layer around what already exists.

# 03 — CREATE TWO DISTINCT BUT CONNECTED EXPERIENCES

## EXPERIENCE A: PUBLIC MARKETING WEBSITE

Purpose: Introduce CINDER, explain its value, demonstrate the experience, establish trust, and convert visitors into attendees.

The public website should feel like a polished consumer social product.

## EXPERIENCE B: AUTHENTICATED APPLICATION HOME

Purpose: Help registered attendees understand what is happening, discover people, participate in their Pod, and navigate their conference.

The authenticated experience should feel like an intuitive social application.

These two experiences must share:

- Brand identity.
- Typography.
- Color system.
- Component language.
- Iconography.
- Interaction conventions.

They must not share the same information hierarchy.

Marketing visitors need product understanding.

Attendees need immediate utility.

# 04 — PUBLIC WEBSITE INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Create a clear marketing-site navigation system.

Suggested header:

**Left:**

- CINDER wordmark and brand identity.

**Center:**

- How It Works.
- Discover.
- Pods.
- AI Facilitator.
- For Conferences.

**Right:**

- Log In.
- Join Conference / Get Started.

Use familiar SaaS and social-product navigation patterns.

Keep the header visually restrained.

Do not overcrowd it.

On desktop, use a clean horizontal navigation structure.

On mobile, use a recognizable responsive navigation menu with accessible controls and clear CTA visibility.

Consider a sticky header if it improves navigation and does not obstruct content.

Use active, hover, focus, and pressed states consistently.

Make the primary CTA visually distinct without making every navigation item look like a button.

# 05 — HERO SECTION: SELL THE EXPERIENCE IMMEDIATELY

The hero is the most important section of the public landing page.

It must establish what CINDER is, why it matters, and what the visitor should do next.

### Suggested headline

**The best part of a conference is who you meet.**

Supporting copy:

*CINDER helps you discover the right people, find your Pod, and turn conference attendance into meaningful connections.*

Primary CTA:

**Join the Experience**

Secondary CTA:

**See How It Works**

Include a visually compelling product presentation.

The hero should communicate the actual CINDER experience through a carefully composed visual.

Potential visual elements:

- Attendee discovery cards.
- Mutual match indicators.
- Pod member avatars.
- A live-looking Pod conversation.
- AI facilitator announcement.
- Conference meetup details.

The visual should tell a story rather than resemble a generic analytics dashboard.

Use the available AI image-generation skill to create original supporting visual assets and explore the art direction.

### Hero composition

Explore a balanced layout:

- Clear visual hierarchy.
