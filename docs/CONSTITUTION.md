# The CINDER Constitution

The fundamental, immutable laws governing the architecture, privacy, intelligence, and facilitation of CINDER.

---

## Preamble

A conference is not its venue, its badges, or its timetable. A conference is the community of human beings who assemble in a single room.

CINDER exists to catalyze meaningful human connections with intention, empathy, and computational precision. To protect attendee agency, ensure technical resilience, and avoid dehumanizing social mechanics, CINDER is governed by six constitutional laws.

---

## The Six Laws

### 1. The Law of Attendee Agency
Technology must never force or coerce social interactions. Every attendee possesses total sovereign control over their visibility, swipe actions, channel participation, and meetup attendance. No attendee is ever compelled to join a Pod or meet in person without their explicit consent.

### 2. The Law of Explainable Relevance
Match recommendations must never be a black box. Whenever CINDER suggests a connection, it must provide a transparent, human-comprehensible rationale explaining the shared focus, complementary technical expertise, or common challenge that prompted the suggestion.

### 3. The Law of Disciplinary Diversity
Pod composition must never collapse into an echo chamber. True conference breakthroughs happen at the intersection of disciplines. CINDER balances semantic affinity with cross-functional diversity, intentionally grouping engineers, researchers, operators, and designers who approach common problems from distinct perspectives.

### 4. The Law of Proactive Yet Humble Facilitation
The AI Facilitator exists to serve the group, not to dominate it. It must be proactive: introducing members, proposing icebreakers, and alerting pods to upcoming sessions. However, its permissions are strictly bounded: it cannot impersonate attendees, invent venue details, or modify master conference agendas. Once human conversation begins, the facilitator steps back into the background.

### 5. The Law of Deterministic Fallback & Zero-Dependency Resilience
At a busy conference center with thousands of connected devices, Wi-Fi fluctuates and external networks fail. CINDER must never become a brick when an external cluster or cloud service disconnects. Core retrieval, vector matching, pod composition, and messaging must function flawlessly with in-process embedded fallbacks.

### 6. The Law of Architectural Sovereignty
Code organization is an architectural contract. Business logic, mathematical retrieval leaf functions, orchestration controllers, external infrastructure adapters, and user interfaces must maintain strict boundaries. Architectural drift must be machine-checkable and blocked at the commit level.
